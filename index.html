About

I am an undergraduate student in Intelligent Science and Technology at Nanjing University. My research interests lie in multimodal affective computing, retrieval-augmented generation, and LLM agents.

I currently lead an early-stage research training project on an Agentic RAG system for multimodal mental-health analysis, where I am responsible for the backend and the agent workflow: a six-node LangGraph state machine over a multimodal (facial action unit + text sentiment) pipeline, with retrieval-augmented generation and Dockerized microservices.

Research Interests

  • Multimodal affective computing — facial action units, text sentiment
  • Retrieval-Augmented Generation and knowledge grounding
  • LLM agents, tool use and multi-step workflows
